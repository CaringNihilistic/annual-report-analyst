Tech Mahindra. Every answer cites the company, fiscal year and page.

Source code, evaluation results and design notes:
https://github.com/CaringNihilistic/annual-report-analyst

This free Space sleeps after 48 hours without visitors; the first visit after that wakes it
(a few minutes: the database is restored on start). Answers come only from the companies'
published annual reports and may contain errors. Nothing here is investment advice.
